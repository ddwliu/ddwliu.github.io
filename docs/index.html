Research

My research connects urban planning, computer vision, and environmental health. Using street view imagery, photorealistic 3D city models, and multi-source urban data, I measure the lived urban environment and investigate its relationships with human health and behavior.

I develop tools to measure urban greenery, biodiversity, human activity, and environmental exposure, with the goal of informing healthier, greener, and more livable cities.
